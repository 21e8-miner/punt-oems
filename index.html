Execution Safeguards
Punt OEMS asks 6 essential questions before executing each child order:
  • Is liquidity > $250K floor?
  • Is DexScreener / GeckoTerminal divergence < 5%?
  • Has Token-2022 transfer fee altered?
  • Has price drifted > 2.5% from arrival price?
  • Is child quote inside maximum impact budget?
  • Did Jupiter order deteriorate before signing?
If any condition is violated, the plan pauses immediately rather than chasing.
